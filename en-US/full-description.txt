Liftbook is a focused offline journal for strength training, cardio, and bodyweight progress. Your data stays on your device, with no account, ads, analytics, or Liftbook server.

Plan and log strength training:
• Build your own exercise library, custom categories, and reusable workout templates
• Search, reorder, duplicate, and delete templates
• Log weight, repetitions, RIR, set comments, and optional local videos with quick step controls
• Review completed workouts, independent weight and repetition records, and progress charts for estimated 1RM, weight, and repetitions
• Keep today's workout editable throughout the day

Record your cardio:
• Add manual activities or use a recoverable foreground timer
• Track only the metrics supported by each activity type
• Reuse templates and review goals, history, and period statistics
• Share privacy-filtered PNG summaries of completed activities

Understand your bodyweight:
• Record daily bodyweight in kilograms or pounds
• Organize entries into weekly averages
• Create lose, maintain, or gain phases with a weekly target
• Attach optional progress photos from your device
• See the current week's average and daily entries in the Android home-screen widget

Back up on your terms:
• Export Bodyweight, Cardio, or Training to separate CSV files
• Restore one area without replacing the other journal data or app preferences

Private by design:
• Your journal stays on your device unless you explicitly export or share it
• Android cloud backup is disabled

Liftbook is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Trends and estimates are for personal logging only. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice and before making health or exercise decisions that may affect you.
